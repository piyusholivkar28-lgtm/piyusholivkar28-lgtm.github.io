MICROHYDRODYNAMICS

Sperm motility from microscopy to simulation

Inferring flagellar kinematics from experimental centrelines and resolving the surrounding Stokes flow with force-coupling methods.
κ0(s,t) → rod mechanics → u(x,t)
02

SPACE PHYSIOLOGY

Cerebral haemodynamics in altered gravity

Mechanistic models connecting cephalad fluid shift, vascular resistance, autoregulation and cerebral blood-flow dynamics under acute microgravity.
3D CFD + RCR + cerebral autoregulation
03

DYNAMICAL SYSTEMS

Circadian–endocrine synchronization

Reduced-order delayed oscillator models for identifying loss of physiological synchronization and early-warning behaviour.
φ̇ = Δω − 2K sin φ(t−τ)
04

METABOLIC PHYSIOLOGY

Muscle-mediated glucose regulation

Physics-informed reduced models linking microgravity-induced muscle loss to glucose–insulin regulation and metabolic risk.
muscle mass ↔ insulin sensitivity
05

FLOW INSTABILITY

Centrifugal instability in toroidal Couette flow

A reduced-order physical framework for understanding how toroidal curvature and differential rotation localize centrifugal instability between rotating toroidal boundaries.
curvature + rotation → localized instability
