  • 01

    Microhydrodynamics

    Sperm motility from microscopy to simulation

    This project begins with measured human-sperm beats rather than an ideal travelling wave. Microscopy centrelines are converted into curvature descriptions, and the Force Coupling Method (FCM) reconstructs the low-Reynolds-number flow and hydrodynamic interactions generated by those beats.

    Experimental input
    Microscopy-derived flagellar centrelines and swimming trajectories from VISEM.
    Beat description
    Frequency, amplitude, asymmetry, curvature and higher-harmonic content.
    Computation
    Stokes equations with FCM for single-, pair- and many-swimmer simulations.
    Readouts
    Flow modes, trajectory prediction, alignment, repulsion, collision tendency and collective transport.
    measured κ(s,t) → FCM flow field → interaction rules
  • 02

    Space physiology

    Cerebral haemodynamics in altered gravity

    A patient-specific carotid-bifurcation model is used to test how acute microgravity changes pulsatile blood delivery to the internal and external carotid branches when vascular resistance, compliance and autoregulatory control are altered together.

    Geometry
    CT-derived three-dimensional carotid bifurcation reconstructed in 3D Slicer.
    Flow model
    Transient 3D CFD with pulsatile flow, Carreau–Yasuda viscosity and grid-independence checks.
    Physiology
    RCR inflow, ICA/ECA resistance outlets, microgravity retuning and cerebral autoregulatory feedback.
    Readouts
    ICA/ECA flow split, pulse amplitude and phase, TAWSS, OSI and relative residence time.
    patient geometry + 3D CFD + RCR + autoregulation
  • 03

    Dynamical systems

    Circadian–endocrine synchronization

    The model asks when the relative timing between the central circadian clock and an adrenal endocrine clock loses resilience. It treats circulating cortisol as an observable output—not as an autonomous 24-hour HPA oscillator—and derives testable signatures of approaching phase-locking breakdown.

    Biological system
    Relative phase between central SCN timing and adrenal endocrine circadian timing.
    Reduction
    Weakly coupled limit cycles reduced to a periodic relative-phase interaction law.
    Instability
    Saddle-node loss of phase locking and a delay-induced Hopf threshold.
    Early warnings
    Critical slowing down, rising variance and autocorrelation, and noise-induced phase slips.
    φ̇ = Δω − Γ sin φ;   |Δω| ≤ Γ
  • 04

    Identifiability & measurement design

    Cardiovascular parameter identifiability

    Can pressure trajectories that are fitted almost perfectly still correspond to the wrong physiological parameters? This study uses a ten-parameter, four-compartment cardiovascular model to derive exact parameter symmetries and test which pressure–flow acquisition protocols break them under matched noise.

    Model
    Ten-parameter, four-compartment passive circulation model with heart rate prescribed as an input.
    Exact ambiguity
    Global volume scaling and pump–peripheral conductance redistribution can leave all pressure trajectories unchanged.
    Protocols tested
    Three pressure traces, timed flow observations and fixed versus varying heart-rate inputs.
    Main finding
    Breaking an exact symmetry can restore local rank without guaranteeing accurate unregularized recovery under noise.
    p(t; θ1) = p(t; θ2)   while   θ1 ≠ θ2
  • 05

    Flow instability

    Centrifugal instability in toroidal Couette flow

    The study isolates how streamline curvature changes the onset and spatial localization of centrifugal instability in the annular gap between concentric toroidal boundaries, with the inner torus rotating and the outer torus fixed.

    Configuration
    Concentric toroidal gap with differential rotation and finite toroidal curvature.
    Analysis
    Computed base flow followed by reduced and full-gap linear-stability eigenproblems.
    Reference limit
    Narrow-gap and Taylor–Couette behaviour used to separate curvature effects.
    Readouts
    Critical Reynolds number, unstable eigenmode and inner/outer-side localization.
    curvature + rotation → critical Re and mode localization
