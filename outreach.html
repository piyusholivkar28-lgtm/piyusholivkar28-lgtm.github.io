Talks & outreach

Research awareness, campus by campus.

Free, direct conversations that help engineering students connect fundamentals with research, higher education and technical careers—beginning in Maharashtra and extending across India.

The initiative · Engineering beyond examinations

Are You Studying Engineering—or Just Passing Exams?

Lessons I Wish I Knew When I Started Engineering

A direct conversation about developing as an engineer: building fundamentals, connecting mathematics and theory with real problems, navigating setbacks, and making informed decisions about technical careers, higher education and research.

I want engineering students—particularly those studying in regional and non-elite institutions—to see engineering as a way of understanding and solving real problems, not merely as a degree or a sequence of examinations.

Designed for students from Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Electrical, Electronics and other core engineering disciplines.

Questions explored

  1. Why do engineering subjects matter beyond examinations?
  2. How can students approach difficult theory, mathematics and numerical problem-solving?
  3. How do engineering fundamentals connect with real applications and technical careers?
  4. Do core engineering disciplines still offer meaningful opportunities?
  5. How should students evaluate industry, GATE, M.Tech., MS, PhD and research?
  6. How can students recover when GATE or an IIT/NIT admission does not work out initially?
